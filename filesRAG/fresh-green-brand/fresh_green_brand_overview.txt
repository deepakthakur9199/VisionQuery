# Fresh Green Brand - Premium Organic Agricultural Produce & Fresh Fruits

Fresh Green Brand is an organic agricultural exporter specializing in tropical fresh fruits, organic avocados, citrus produce, and sustainable farm-to-table supply chain logistics.

## Key Produce & Product Line

1. **Hass Avocado**: Organic, rich-flavored creamy Hass avocados sourced directly from high-altitude sustainable orchards.
2. **Fresh Strawberries**: Hand-picked sweet organic strawberries cultivated under eco-friendly greenhouse conditions.
3. **Tahiti Lime**: Juicy, seedless fresh green Tahiti limes harvested at peak acidity and freshness.
4. **Fresh Mango**: Sweet Kent and Tommy Atkins tropical mangoes packed with natural vitamin C and essential antioxidants.
5. **Fresh Pineapple**: Premium Golden MD2 sweet pineapples known for their high Brix sweetness and vibrant aroma.
6. **Lulo Fruit (Naranjilla)**: Exquisite exotic South American citrus fruit widely used for gourmet beverages and fresh juices.

## Company Performance & Financial Highlights
Fresh Green Brand recorded strong financial growth driven by expanding export trade agreements in North America and Western Europe, achieving double-digit net revenue gains and expanding sustainable organic farming operations across certified partner farms.
